Cartera CL

WebPWAIn development

Positions, returns, a dividend calendar, income goals and technical analysis for investors in Chile. It doesn't buy or sell: it only records and analyses.

Email me

What you can do

  • Positions and returnsAverage cost, realised gains and time-weighted return against the IPSA.
  • DividendsA calendar with record and payment dates, telling announced and projected apart.
  • Income goalsHow much your portfolio pays you each month and how far you are from your goal.
  • Technical analysisCandlestick charts with indicators and signals.
  • In pesos and UFDaily prices, UF, dollar and CPI updated automatically.
  • InstallableUse it in the browser or add it to your phone's home screen.
