Bookloft privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what information Bookloft, developed by KbzonZito, handles.

Data collected

None. Bookloft has no accounts and no server of its own. Your library, notes and files are stored only on your device.

Connections

The app only connects to Open Library and Apple Books, to search for books and covers; to Anthropic, only if you add your own API key; and wherever you choose to share a backup (Drive, email, files).

Your API key

If you use the AI features, your Anthropic API key is stored encrypted in the phone's Keystore and is never included in backups. Images you send to Claude are covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

Only used when you scan an ISBN or pick a photo, and the image stays on your phone unless you use the AI with your API key.

Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

Any change will be published on this page with its update date.

Contact
