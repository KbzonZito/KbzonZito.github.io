Printer Hub privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what information Printer Hub, developed by KbzonZito, handles.

Data collected

None. Printer Hub has no account, analytics, ads or server of its own. Your printers, settings and saved previews stay on your phone, and you can delete them in Settings → Storage.

Connections

The app only connects to the printers you add, directly on your local network or over your own VPN.

Permissions

Internet and network state, to talk to your printers. Notifications and a foreground service, to keep you posted on a print while the app is closed.

Backups

If you export a backup, the file is saved wherever you choose. The app never sends it anywhere.

Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

Any change will be published on this page with its update date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email mvergaraguerrero@proton.me.
